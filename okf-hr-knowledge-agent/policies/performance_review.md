## Performance Review Policy

All employees undergo a formal performance review twice a year: June and December.

## Self Assessment

Employees must complete a self-assessment form two weeks before the review date.

## Rating Scale

- Exceeds Expectations
- Meets Expectations
- Needs Improvement

## Outcome

Reviews inform salary adjustments, promotions and development plans.
