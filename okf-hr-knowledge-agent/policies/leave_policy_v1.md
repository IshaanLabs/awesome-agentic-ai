## Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 15 days of annual leave per calendar year.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 7 days of paid sick leave per year.

## Carry Forward

No carry forward of unused leave is permitted.

## Applies To

All full-time employees hired before 2024.
