## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to act with integrity, respect and professionalism.

## Respect in the Workplace

Harassment, discrimination or bullying of any kind will not be tolerated.

## Confidentiality

Employees must not share confidential company information with external parties.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any potential conflicts of interest to their manager.

## Reporting Violations

Violations can be reported anonymously via the HR portal or directly to HR.

## Consequences

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
