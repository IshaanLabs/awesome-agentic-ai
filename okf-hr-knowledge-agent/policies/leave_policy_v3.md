## Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregiver: 26 weeks paid. Secondary caregiver: 4 weeks paid.

## Carry Forward

Up to 5 days of unused leave may be carried forward to the next year.

## Applies To

All full-time employees. Effective from January 2026.
