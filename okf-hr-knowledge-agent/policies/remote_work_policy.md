## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed 6 months of employment.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop. Internet allowance of $50/month is reimbursable.

## Core Hours

Employees must be available between 10am - 3pm in their local timezone.
