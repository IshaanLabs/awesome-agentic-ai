## Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year.

## Carry Forward

Up to 3 days of unused leave may be carried forward to the next year.

## Applies To

All full-time employees hired before 2025.
