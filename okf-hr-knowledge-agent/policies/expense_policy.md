## Expense Reimbursement Policy

All business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of incurrence.

## Travel

- Flights: Economy class for domestic, business class for international over 8 hours
- Hotels: Up to $200/night domestic, $300/night international
- Meals: Up to $50/day

## Remote Work Expenses

- Internet: Up to $50/month
- Home office setup: One-time $500 allowance

## Approval

Expenses under $500 require manager approval. Over $500 require director approval.
